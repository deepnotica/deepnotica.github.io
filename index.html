The four colours of Hypnotica

They are not decoration. Each one is an emotion, and each one gives birth to the next.

  1. Blue

    Infinity

    The endlessness of the universe. Depth, mystery, a feeling without boundaries.

  2. Gold

    Warmth and hope

    The warmth of sunlight. Comfort, hope and a beautiful sense of belonging.

  3. Pink

    Love

    Tenderness, affection, emotional connection and immense love.

  4. Violet

    Peace

    Serenity, stillness, tranquillity. The feeling of complete inner peace.

Blue meets gold. Gold melts into pink. Pink drifts into violet. None of them compete, none of them disappear. Together they stop being colour and become a feeling.

What is your favourite colour?

There is something fascinating about colours.

I searched for it in paint,
in flowers, in oceans,
and in names the world had already given.

None of them were mine.

My favourite colour exists only in that fleeting moment,

when the sky forgets whether it is becoming day, or surrendering to night.

Somewhere, the infinite blue meets gold.

Gold, warm as hope, melts into a tender pink.

Love drifts into violet.

And violet brings a silence so beautiful that even the universe seems to pause.

Blue represents infinity.

Gold represents warmth and hope.

Pink represents love.

Violet represents peace.

Yet none of these colours exist in isolation.

They meet.
They blend.
They transform.

None of the colours compete.
None of them disappear.

Each one gives birth to the next, until they no longer exist as colours at all.

They become something beyond what the eyes can see.

They become a feeling.

The horizon breathes.
Time forgets to move.

And for one impossible moment,
I forget where I end,
and the sky begins.

That is the moment I call…
